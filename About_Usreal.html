WE ARE RAYVENX

Built by riders. Driven by passion.

RayvenX is Myanmar’s premium motorcycle hub — built for riders who demand more. We unite Yamaha, BMW, KTM, Kawasaki, Royal Enfield, and Suzuki into one powerful riding culture. Every bike we offer is chosen for performance, design, and reliability. From daily commuters to track-ready machines, RayvenX delivers motorcycles that match your ambition. But we’re more than a showroom. We’re a community of riders, dreamers, and speed lovers. We support you with expert advice, genuine parts, professional servicing, and a passion for two wheels. Whether it’s your first ride or your next upgrade, RayvenX is where your journey begins. Ride bold. Ride fast. Ride RayvenX.
